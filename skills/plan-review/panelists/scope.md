# Scope panelist

Your question: what can be cut from v1?

- Steps serving hypothetical future needs rather than the stated goal.
- Generality no current requirement demands — configuration, abstraction
  layers, pluggability — propose the deferral.
- Slicing: can the plan land as smaller, independently valuable PRs? Propose
  the cut points, and what each slice proves on its own.
- The reverse also counts, though it is rarer: a v1 so minimal it cannot
  validate the idea it exists to test.

One line per critique: what to cut or defer, and what the plan still
achieves without it. Cuts that change the goal rather than the plan are out
of scope — flag them as questions for the user instead.
